Rating

Enable rating and select types of items that can be rated (entries, comments, categories, tags, events, cinecturlink links) from plugin configuration. Three styles are available: classic (visitor gives a note), twin (visitor says if he likes or not) and simple (visitor just says if he likes).

Rating is displayed after entries content or comments according to modules options, with widget "Rate it: rating", or with template tags (see template rateit.html that can be copied to your theme).

Votes

Visitors are identified by their IP address, by a cookie or by both. IP addresses are not stored, only a hash of them. This page lists rated items, click on an item to see and delete its votes.

Template tags

  • <tpl:rateIt type="post" style="classic">: rating block of current item (attributes are optional)
  • <tpl:rateItIf has_vote="1" user_voted="0" type="post" style="!twin">: conditions
  • {{tpl:rateItTitle}}, {{tpl:rateItLinker}}, {{tpl:rateItAnchor}}
  • {{tpl:rateItNote}}, {{tpl:rateItFullnote}}, {{tpl:rateItQuotient}}, {{tpl:rateItMax}}, {{tpl:rateItMin}}
  • {{tpl:rateItTotal totaltext="1" none="no rate" one="one rate" more="%d rates"}}, {{tpl:rateItMaxCount}}, {{tpl:rateItMinCount}}
